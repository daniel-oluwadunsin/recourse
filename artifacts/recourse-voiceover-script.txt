Recourse is an AI case-intelligence agent for difficult institutional decisions. It starts with what happened, understands the record, and helps find a grounded next step.

The flow is simple: bring the decision, see the whole case, and choose what fits.

Recourse researches, analyzes, and drafts while I stay in control of every action outside the product.
